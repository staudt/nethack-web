# Deviations from NetHack

Every intentional rule difference from NetHack is logged here.

| Area                                   | NetHack behavior                                                                                                                                                                  | Our behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Reason                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RNG                                    | `rn2(x)` uses `RND() % x` (modulo bias)                                                                                                                                           | Rejection sampling, no modulo bias                                                                                                                                                                           | Uniform distribution; outcome sequences differ anyway (ref: src/rnd.c)                                    |
| Level generation                       | Locked doors and trapped doors (and door mimics)                                                                                                                                  | Locked doors are made with NetHack's rolls; trapped doors and door mimics are not made (their rolls are still drawn, so the stream matches)                                                                                                                                                    | Trapped doors and mimics are deferred (docs/ROADMAP.md) (ref: src/mklev.c dosdoor)                     |
| Level generation                       | Boulders in blocked extra corridors                                                                                                                                               | No boulders; their rn2(50) roll is skipped                                                                                                                                                                   | Objects are not implemented (ref: src/sp_lev.c dig_corridor)                                              |
| Level generation                       | MAXNROFROOMS is 40; MAXRECT is COLNO*ROWNO/30                                                                                                                                     | Room cap is `max(40, floor(40 * w*h / (80*21)))`; the rect cap already scaled with area                                                                                                                      | Large maps would otherwise stop at 40 rooms (ref: include/global.h, src/rect.c init_rect)                 |
| Level generation                       | dig_corridor gives up after 500 steps                                                                                                                                             | Cap is `max(500, 2*(w+h))`                                                                                                                                                                                   | 500 is too short for forced joins on very large maps (ref: src/sp_lev.c dig_corridor)                     |
| Level generation                       | Vaults, niches (`make_niches()`, some with a trap door), themed rooms, subrooms, special rooms are made; stairs depend on dungeon level (no upstairs on level 1)                                                           | Only ordinary rooms and corridors; both staircases always exist (up in another room when possible, else a different cell of the same room)                                                                   | Not needed for M1 (ref: src/mklev.c makelevel, generate_stairs)                                           |
| Level generation                       | Rooms may end up disconnected (failed forced joins)                                                                                                                               | After makecorridors, any stray room is joined by a deterministic breadth-first corridor with plain doorways (no RNG)                                                                                         | Guarantees the "all rooms connected" invariant (ref: src/mklev.c makecorridors)                           |
| Level generation                       | Map is 79 usable columns (x = 0 unused), min size undefined                                                                                                                       | 0-based coordinates; internally x+1 is used so placement matches NetHack; minimum size 10x10 (smaller never places a room: create_room needs a 9-row free rectangle)                                         | Any size must work; NetHack's rect.c limits make smaller maps impossible                                  |
| Vision                                 | `clear_path()` Bresenham-style line-of-sight tables                                                                                                                               | Symmetric recursive shadowcasting; seen iff in LOS and (lit or adjacent)                                                                                                                                     | Symmetric, artifact-free, fast on large maps (ref: src/vision.c vision_recalc())                          |
| Light sources | `do_light_sources()` lights cells in a source's circle that `clear_path()` reaches from the source | The same circles (`circle_data`), but reach from a source off the hero's cell uses shadowcasting from that cell | Consistent with the hero's line of sight (ref: src/light.c do_light_sources()) |
| Doors                                  | Opening a door can fail ("This door is stuck") based on `rnl(20)` vs Str/Dex/Con                                                                                                  | Opening always succeeds, by walking into the door or with the Open action                                                                                                                                                                                    | Attributes don't exist yet (ref: src/lock.c doopen_indir())                                               |
| Hero | Starting kit: Valkyrie[] items, the dagger becomes the alternate weapon, food ration quantity can be 2 from mksobj | Real objects (+1 spear wielded, dagger as the alternate weapon, +3 small shield worn, food ration); the ration is always 1 | Fixed quantity keeps the kit predictable (ref: src/u_init.c ini_inv) |
| Armor delay | `oc_delay` turns pass before the armor takes effect: AC and `known` change when `Armor_on` runs | The armor is worn and AC changes at once; the hero is then helpless for `oc_delay` turns (monsters act) with the same finishing message | Simpler state; the time cost is the same (ref: src/do_wear.c accessory_or_armor_on, Armor_on) |
| Pick up | Autopickup per options; `pile_limit` option; menus ordered by class; `pickup_burden` option; scare monster / cockatrice corpse checks | Autopickup is off (NetHack 5.0 default); `pile_limit` 5 and `pickup_burden` Stressed are fixed; menu lists the pile top first; no special-object checks; arriving by stairs prints no 'You see here' | Options and special objects are not implemented (ref: src/pickup.c pickup, lift_object, src/invent.c look_here) |
| Inventory display | Gold is `$` in the inventory; discoveries list in order of discovery with the bare description ('puce') | Gold is `$`; discoveries list in object-list order with the full appearance phrase ('puce potion', 'scroll labeled FOO') | Appearance names are whole phrases in the catalog (ref: src/o_init.c dodiscovered) |
| Wielding | Any item can be wielded; `x` swaps primary and alternate weapons (also with two-weapon combat); quiver | Only weapon-class items are offered; the alternate weapon is content state (`modData.nh.uswapwep`, not a core equip slot) and `x` swaps as in `doswapweapon()` without two-weapon combat; wielding the alternate weapon swaps; no quiver; 'Wield nothing' is not offered once the wielded weapon is known to be cursed; `x` is offered only while a weapon is wielded or alternate | The quiver arrives with throwing; core has no alternate slot (ref: src/wield.c dowield, doswapweapon, setuswapwep) |
| Search | `dosearch0()` uses `rnl(7 - fund)` with Luck, lenses/artifact bonus, and also finds hidden monsters and traps | Secret doors and corridors, and traps (`!rnl(8)`, "You find a bear trap."), with Luck and no lenses or artifact bonus; a found secret door becomes a closed or a locked door, as it was made; hidden monsters are not looked for | Lenses, artifacts and hiding monsters do not exist yet (ref: src/detect.c dosearch0(), find_trap()) |

| Prayer troubles | `in_trouble()` also finds stoning, sliming, lava, clouds, lycanthropy, collapsing and lost attributes (ABASE < AMAX), walls, levitation, blindfolds, punishment, fumbling, saddles and deafness | Strangling, sickness, Weak or Hungry, low HP, a welded weapon, cursed worn items (armor, amulet, rings), blindness, wounded legs, stun, confusion and hallucination | Attribute peaks (AMAX) and the other conditions are not modelled (ref: src/pray.c in_trouble()) |
| Travel                                 | `findtravelpath()` falls back to guess mode (walks to the reachable cell nearest an unreachable target); `_` also offers `<` `>` shortcuts                                        | Unreachable/unknown targets are not offered and performing one costs no time; the path is re-planned every step over remembered map via `query.pathTo`                                                       | Simplification for M2 (ref: src/hack.c findtravelpath(), packages/content/src/travel.ts)                  |
