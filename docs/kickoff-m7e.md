# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 7e (draft)

Milestone 7e picks up the content-only leftovers of 7d: **digging with a pick-axe or mattock** (the `dig()` occupation, dwarves tunnelling, pits dug from inside a pit and conjoined pits), **rolling boulder traps** (launch spots, `launch_obj()`, Sokoban's spots), **trapped boxes and doors** (`chest_trap()`, door booby traps, `#force`), **`#untrap`**, **land mines with `scatter()`** and **breaking wands with `explode()`** (and cancellation blowing up magical traps). Shop damage and webs come last and may be deferred (see "Deferred if the budget runs out"). It builds on 5b (traps, `docs/kickoff-m5b.md`), 6b (shops, the Watch), 7b (boulders, pits, Sokoban, `docs/kickoff-m7b.md`), 7d (the wand of digging, `dighole()`, levitation, the magic traps, `docs/kickoff-m7d.md`) and m8. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m7e.md`).

Everything here is content, plus a small `ui` change (the explosion's box glyphs) and one small optional core field (`Level.trappedDoorSpots`, transient like `boulderSpots`; open question 1). No save-format change. What needs a real core contract change stays in 7f.

## Scope

1. **Pick-axe and mattock:** the pick-axe object (`objects.h` order, its tool probability; dwarves already roll for it), `#apply` asks "In what direction do you want to dig?" (the eight directions, `<` "You cannot reach the ceiling.", `>`); applying wields it; `dig()` as a resumable occupation ("You continue digging." / "You start digging."): rock to corridor, room walls to doorways (corridor on cavernous levels, floor on maze levels), closed doors broken, boulders fractured, trees cut, "This wall is too hard to dig into." on non-diggable walls; down: a pit after effort 50 ("you dig a pit in the floor"), a hole after 250 (falling through, `dighole()`); the Watch warns, then gets angry; the shopkeeper's "leave your pick-axe outside" counts pick-axes too (ref: include/objects.h PICK_AXE; src/dig.c use_pick_axe(), use_pick_axe2(), dig(), dig_typ(), dig_check(), holetime(), watch_dig() !zap; src/apply.c doapply() PICK_AXE/DWARVISH_MATTOCK; src/shk.c u_entered_shop()).
2. **Pits:** digging sideways from inside a pit digs an adjacent pit and conjoins them (pick-axe and the wand of digging); she moves between conjoined pits without climbing out; filling or removing a pit clears its links (ref: src/dig.c adj_pit_checks(), zap_dig() pit branch, use_pick_axe2(); src/hack.c test_move()/domove() conjoined pits; src/trap.c clear_conjoined_pits(), join_adjacent_pits()).
3. **Dwarves dig:** monsters that need a pick wield one to dig (`m_digweapon_check()`), move into rock and walls (`mfndpos` ALLOW_DIG) and tunnel (`mdig_tunnel()`: doors broken, walls to doorways or corridor, rock to corridor, "You hear the sound of crashing rock."), never in Sokoban or through non-diggable walls (ref: src/dig.c mdig_tunnel(); src/monmove.c m_digweapon_check(), m_move() can_tunnel; src/mon.c mfndpos(); src/weapon.c mon_wield_item() NEED_PICK_AXE).
4. **Rolling boulder traps:** the trap kind (from level 2 in the random roll), `mkroll_launch()` placing its boulder and launch spots, `launch_obj()` rolling the boulder through monsters, the hero, pits and holes, teleporters, closed doors and other boulders; monsters set them off; Sokoban's ten spots get the trap with no boulder ("Fortunately for you, no boulder was released.") (ref: src/trap.c trapeffect_rolling_boulder_trap(), launch_obj(), mkroll_launch(), find_random_launch_coord(), isclearpath(); src/mklev.c traptype_rnd(); dat/soko*.lua).
5. **Trapped boxes and doors, `#force`:** boxes made trapped by the roll already drawn (`otrapped`), `chest_trap()` when one is opened, looted or its lock picked; doors made trapped by the rolls already drawn ("KABOOM!!  The door was booby-trapped!", `b_trapped()`) when opened, unlocked, kicked or struck, and monsters opening them; `#force` with a wielded weapon on a locked box (prying with a blade that may break, bashing with a blunt weapon that may wreck the box and its potions); the wish parser's "trapped" (ref: src/trap.c chest_trap(), b_trapped(); src/lock.c doforce(), forcelock(), picklock(), doopen_indir(), boxlock(), doorlock(); src/monmove.c postmov() mb_trapped(); src/dokick.c dokick() door branch; src/mkobj.c mksobj_init(); src/mklev.c dosdoor(); src/sp_lev.c create_door(); src/shknam.c stock_room()).
6. **Land mines and `scatter()`:** the trap kind (from level 6), "KAABLAMM!!!  You triggered a land mine!" (`rnd(16)`, wounded legs), `blow_up_landmine()` leaving a pit, monsters, boulders pushed or rolled onto one; `scatter()` throwing the objects around (hitting, breaking, fracturing), which also closes the kicked-coins and bag-of-holding deviations (ref: src/trap.c trapeffect_landmine(), blow_up_landmine(); src/explode.c scatter(); src/hack.c moverock() LANDMINE; src/dokick.c kick_object coins; src/pickup.c do_boh_explosion()).
7. **`#untrap`:** floor traps here or next to her (arrow and dart traps give up their ammunition, bear traps and land mines become objects, squeaky boards need oil), failing sets the trap off; helping a trapped monster out of a pit or bear trap, pets and peacefuls; boxes ("You find a trap on the chest!") and doors (ref: src/trap.c dountrap(), untrap(), untrap_prob(), try_disarm(), cnv_trap_obj(), disarm_*(), help_monster_out(), untrap_box(), disarm_box(); include/objects.h BEARTRAP, LAND_MINE).
8. **Breaking wands and `explode()`:** applying a wand breaks it ("Raising your wand high above your head, you break it in two!", after a yes/no), with `do_break_wand()`'s cases (nothing else happens, magical/fiery/frosty explosions, striking's wall of force, digging's ring of pits and holes, the beam effects on the eight squares and on her); `explode()` for the hero and monsters with resistances and item destruction; a cancellation beam blows up magical traps, and digging down on one does too (ref: src/apply.c do_break_wand(), broken_wand_explode(); src/explode.c explode(); src/zap.c zap_map(), maybe_explode_trap(); src/dig.c dighole()).

## Deferred if the budget runs out (plan steps 8 and 9)

- **Shop damage:** `add_damage()`/`pay_for_damage()` for walls and doors dug, kicked or exploded in a shop ("Please pay for the door!"), the shopkeeper repairing them over time, `shopdig()` ("Be careful, sir, or you might fall through the floor.", the backpack grabbed when she falls) (ref: src/shk.c add_damage(), pay_for_damage(), repair_damage(), shk_fixes_damage(), litter_scatter(), shopdig(); src/dokick.c).
- **Webs:** the web trap and spiders (the giant spider `mktrap()` puts on a web, `maybe_spin_web()`), escaping and tearing webs; needs the spider monsters (a roster change), so it most likely moves to a later milestone (ref: src/trap.c trapeffect_web(), mu_maybe_destroy_web(); src/monmove.c maybe_spin_web(); src/hack.c domove_fight_web()).

## Out of scope

Setting traps with a beartrap or land mine object (`use_trap()`); kicking boxes (`container_impact_dmg()`, "WHAMM!!"); burying and unearthing objects; liquid flow into holes (no pools); drawbridges, graves and thrones; the scroll of charging and `wand_explode()`; engraving with wands; the Master Key of Thievery; polymorph and statues (7f); `cursed_book()`'s explosion as `explode()` (it stays as is).

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`: wish for a pick-axe, dig through rock, a wall and a door, dig down to a pit and a hole, dig an adjacent pit from inside a pit and walk between them; watch a dwarf tunnel; wish for and step on a rolling boulder trap and a land mine; open a trapped chest and a trapped door; `#force` a locked box; `#untrap` an arrow trap and a bear trap and help a pet out of a pit; break a wand of fire and a wand of digging; zap cancellation at a magic trap.
- In a normal game the new traps turn up at their usual depths, dwarves carry and use pick-axes, Sokoban has its rolling boulder spots.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic (with an explained golden update for the pick-axe object and any other cause the plan names), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.
