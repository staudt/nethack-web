# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 22 (draft)

Milestone 22 brings NetHack's **13 roles and 5 races** to the port. Today the hero is always a human, female, neutral Valkyrie. After m22 a new game asks for a role, race, gender and alignment, and each choice gets NetHack's starting inventory, attributes, hit points and energy, intrinsics by experience level, skills and `#enhance` limits, role spells, gods, and the race and role rules that change play. The quest is out.

The milestone comes in two parts. **m22a (steps 1-7) is content only.** It needs no answer to the open questions. It ports the role and race tables, the missing kit objects, the starting inventory of every role and race, alignment and pantheons, and the role and race rules. While m22a runs, a new game is still the Valkyrie, and other roles exist only for tests. **m22b (steps 8-10)** adds character creation: core role and race defs, the new-game options, the save format, and the menus in the app. Each m22b step depends on costly-to-undo questions (plan Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5). If those are parked, m22a still stands alone: the Valkyrie becomes NetHack-exact, and every other role is ready to switch on.

m22 branches off the m21 tip. It does not depend on m20 (artifacts, parked): quest artifacts, sacrifice gifts and Excalibur stay out. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (Mods, State, Worker protocol, the polymorphed-hero note D5) and `DEVIATIONS.md` (Hero, Weapon skills, Quit and new game, Monster generation, Prayer, Pets), then the plan `~/.claude/plans/m22.md`.

## Scope

### m22a: roles and races in content (no core change)

1. **Role and race tables** from `src/role.c`: the 13 roles' data. That covers the male and female names, rank titles, gods, file codes, allowed races, genders and alignments, base attributes and their distribution, hit point and energy advancement, `xlev`, `initrecord`, the spellcasting stats and the special spell. The 5 races have names, adjectives, file codes, allowed alignments, self/love/hate masks, attribute limits, and hit point and energy advancement. From `src/attrib.c`, each role's and race's intrinsics by level (`arc_abil[]` .. `wiz_abil[]`, `dwa_abil[]` .. `hum_abil[]`). From `src/u_init.c`, the skill limits (`Skill_A[]` .. `Skill_W[]`). The hero's attributes, hit points, energy, intrinsics, skill limits and spell success all read these tables (ref: src/attrib.c init_attr(), rnd_attr(), init_attr_role_redist(), vary_init_attr(), newhp(), adjabil(); src/exper.c newpw(), enermod(); src/spell.c percent_success()).
2. **The objects the kits need:** stethoscope, expensive camera, leash, saddle, bell, fortune cookie, orange and pancake, with their uses: listening with the stethoscope (free once per turn), the camera's flash, leashing a pet, ringing the bell, the fortune in the cookie (ref: src/apply.c use_stethoscope(), use_camera(), use_bell(); src/dog.c/apply.c use_leash(), check_leash(); src/eat.c fortune cookie, src/rumors.c outrumor(); include/objects.h).
3. **Starting inventory for every role and race:** each role's kit and its random extras, race substitutions (elven, orcish and dwarvish gear; gnomish crossbows), random items filtered as NetHack does, starting gold, known object classes, wielded, alternate and worn items, starting spells, the energy floor for a spellcaster, the carry boost, and the role's pet (kitten, little dog or pony with a saddle). The Valkyrie's kit becomes exact (the oil lamp 1 time in 6, the kitten or little dog, the known weapon and armour classes) (ref: src/u_init.c u_init_role(), u_init_race(), ini_inv(), ini_inv_mkobj_filter(), ini_inv_obj_substitution(), ini_inv_adjust_obj(), ini_inv_use_obj(), knows_object(), knows_class(), u_init_carry_attr_boost(), u_init_skills_discoveries(); src/weapon.c skill_init(); src/spell.c initialspell(); src/dog.c makedog(), pet_type()).
4. **Alignment and gods:** each role's pantheon, and the Priest's random pantheon. Gods and goddesses appear in prayer, altars and messages. The starting alignment record comes from the role. Lawful and chaotic heroes get their own branches of holy and unholy water, prayer and altars, and the original alignment is remembered (ref: src/role.c role_init() pantheon; src/pray.c align_gname(), align_gtitle(); src/potion.c peffect_water(); src/attrib.c newhp() initrecord).
5. **Race rules:** peaceful and hostile races (`always_peaceful()`/`always_hostile()` from the race masks; a dwarf or gnome hero among peaceful Mines inhabitants), racial intrinsics (infravision, an orc's poison resistance, an elf's sleep resistance), cannibalism (`your_race()`, Cavemen and orcs exempt), racial food (lembas and cram), racial multishot and object knowledge, racial attribute limits, and a dwarf's faster digging (ref: src/makemon.c peace_minded(); src/eat.c maybe_cannibal(), lembas/cram nutrition; src/dothrow.c multishot race bonus; src/dig.c dig()).
6. **Role rules in combat:** martial arts for Monks and Samurai (damage, the staggering blow, Master and Grand Master), the Monk's armour and weapon penalties and his to-hit bonus, the Knight's code of conduct ("You caitiff!") and the Samurai's ("You dishonorably attack the innocent!"), the Rogue's backstab, role multishot bonuses, and the Knight's jumping (ref: src/uhitm.c find_roll_to_hit(), hmon_hitmon(), check_caitiff(); src/weapon.c weapon_hit_bonus(), weapon_dam_bonus(); src/dothrow.c multishot_class_bonus(); src/dokick.c martial()).
7. **The other role rules:** the Priest sees blessed and cursed objects, the Samurai uses Japanese object names, the Archeologist digs faster and feels guilty breaking historic statues, the Knight is a glutton when he overeats, the Monk is a vegetarian, the Healer and the Wizard have their spell rules, the Priest casts light as a clerical spell, books are identified by skill, and each role has its own Hello and Goodbye. The status line shows the rank title and alignment (ref: src/objnam.c, src/invent.c, src/dig.c, src/eat.c, src/spell.c, src/role.c Hello(), Goodbye(), src/botl.c rank_of(), xlev_to_rank()).

### m22b: character creation (needs plan Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5)

8. **Roles and races as defs:** the 13 roles and 5 races registered through `ModApi`, the hero's chosen role, race, gender and alignment in `GameState`, a new game started with a character, the character recorded in replays and saves, and existing saves still loading (ref: src/role.c role_init(), ok_role(), ok_race(), ok_gend(), ok_align(); src/allmain.c newgame()).
9. **Choosing a character:** Play asks "Shall I pick character's race, role, gender and alignment for you?", then role, race, gender and alignment menus filtered by what each allows, with "random" and a final "Is this ok?". The choices can be stored as device options, set by URL parameters for testing, and they apply in Wizard mode too. NetHack's welcome ("You are a neutral female human Valkyrie."), the score list's `Val-Hum-Fem-Neu` for every role, and the attributes screen's background lines follow (ref: src/role.c genl_player_setup(), the `role`/`race`/`gender`/`align` options; src/allmain.c welcome(); src/insight.c background_enlightenment()).
10. **Soak and docs:** the soak bot plays every role and race, then DEVIATIONS, ROADMAP, ARCHITECTURE and the phone check.

## Out of scope

The quest (leaders, nemeses, guardians, the quest artifacts, the portal and their role_init fix-ups); artifacts and sacrifice gifts (m20); riding (the Knight's pony comes saddled but cannot be ridden; riding skill starts at Basic); two-weapon combat (`#twoweapon`); the pauper, nudist and reroll options; pet names (Hachi, Idefix, Sirius...) and the `pettype` option; the player's name in game state; hero glyphs by race or gender.

## Exit criteria

- **m22a:** a new game is still the Valkyrie, now exactly as NetHack starts her (lamp, pet, known classes). Tests show every role's and race's starting kit, attributes, hit points, energy, skills, intrinsics by level, spells and rules matching `vendor/nethack`.
- **m22b:** on a phone I can start a game as each of the 13 roles with any allowed race, gender and alignment (or let the game pick), see my kit, my rank title and alignment on the status line, use `#enhance` within the role's limits, and cast my starting spells. As a dwarf in the Mines the gnomes and dwarves are mostly peaceful. An existing Valkyrie save still loads and plays.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic, with explained golden updates in steps 2 and 3 (and step 8 if the role ids are renamed); `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests: every role's and race's table rows against role.c, attrib.c and u_init.c; `initialAttributes()` stays within the race's limits and totals 75 before variation, for every allowed role and race pair; `newhp()`/`newpw()` per role and race; `adjabil()` across levels 1-30 per role and race; skill limits and starting skill levels per role; `ini_inv` per role and race (items, enchantments, BUC, quantities in range, substitutions, worn, wielded and alternate, discoveries, spells known, gold); role/race/gender/alignment validity (`ok_role()` etc.) for every combination.
- Scenario tests: the role and race rules (a Monk's martial arts, a Knight's caitiff penalty, a Priest seeing BUC, a Samurai's names, a dwarf among peaceful gnomes, an orc eating an orc, a lawful hero's prayer and holy water); new games started with a character; the default game equal to the old Valkyrie; an old v8 Valkyrie save loading.
- The soak bot plays every role and race (step 10). New invariants: the hero's race, gender and alignment are allowed by her role; no skill is above its role maximum; worn and wielded starting items are in her inventory.
- The phone check uses the character menus and `?wizard=1` (with `?role=`, `?race=`, `?gender=`, `?align=` for quick starts).
