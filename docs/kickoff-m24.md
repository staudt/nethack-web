# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 24

Milestone 24 is **the quest**: the magic portal six or seven levels below the Oracle, the quest dungeon behind it with each role's home, locate and goal levels and their fillers, the quest leaders, guardians and nemeses, the leader's entry rules (experience level 14, a pure alignment record, the expulsion and the banishment), the quest texts, the nemesis carrying the Bell of Opening, the quest artifact on the goal level and its return to the leader. The four quest artifacts whose base objects were missing come with their bases (the crystal ball, the mirror and the lenses, with their uses). Ten steps in two parts: **m24a** (steps 1-6: the rows, the objects, the quest dungeon with the Valkyrie's levels, the texts, the leader's rules, the nemesis and the artifact) and **m24b** (steps 7-10: the other twelve roles' levels, three roles a step). Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m24.md`).

**Four small additive core edits and one save bump.** (1) Step 1: `MonsterSound` gains `'leader' | 'nemesis' | 'guardian'` (monsters.h's MS_LEADER, MS_NEMESIS, MS_GUARDIAN). (2) Step 3: `DungeonEntryDef.stairs` gains `'portal'` (dungeon.lua's branchtype "portal", C's BR_PORTAL): no stairs at either end, and the child dungeon starts at its portal level's depth ("portals stay on the same depth"); content places the two magic portals. Fort Ludios switches to it, so its depth becomes NetHack's 18-21. (3) Step 3: `SpecialLevelDef.roleVariants` and `DungeonDef.roleFiller`, the hero's role choosing a special level's LevelDef and the filler above and below a split level (C's "x-strt" names with the role's file code, and `"%s-fila"`/`"%s-filb"` above and below the locate level). (4) Step 3: `ctx.goToLevel(to, { trap })`, arriving on the first trap of that kind once the level is made, else at random (C's arrival on the corresponding magic portal; two home levels put their portal anywhere in a rectangle). `SAVE_VERSION` goes from 10 to 11 in step 3 (the quest dungeon is a new layout; older saves are refused, the user's rule: wiped, not migrated). Everything else is content.

**The Bell of Opening.** As in NetHack the quest nemesis is born with the Bell (makemon()'s MS_NEMESIS item), so from step 3 a Valkyrie can win it in a normal game and the invocation, the Sanctum and the endgame are reachable outside wizard mode (m23's open question 1 ends here). The leader does not hand it out: on the artifact's return without the Bell the leader says so ("quest_complete_no_bell").

**The texts are generated.** `dat/quest.lua` (3100 lines of messages for the 13 roles) becomes two content files written by a new converter target, `pnpm convert:quest`: `questtxt.ts` (which message each role has, its output kind, its synopsis) and `quest.en.ts` (the English, one key per message, with questpgr.c's `%` codes as `{param}` placeholders and their suffixes as ui modifiers). The ui gains four generic modifiers (`|C` capitalized, `|s`/`|S` possessive, `|t` without "the").

**Roles, artifacts and the endgame exist.** Roles and races are m22's (`urole()`, `roleIs()`, the rank titles, the gods); the artifact table with nine quest rows, `oname()`, touching, `#invoke` and the wish refusal are m20's; the Bell of Opening, the invocation and the Planes are m23's; magic portals (`portal.ts`, `MAGIC_PORTAL`, `domagicportal()`) are m17's and Fort Ludios's branch handling m21's.

## Out of scope

The `legacy` introduction at new game, livelog and achievements, `#overview`/mapseen (`remdun_mapseen()`), bones, the nemesis's stinking cloud (no gas-cloud regions), talking artifacts (the Magic Mirror of Merlin's speech), monster genders and names ("Vimes", "the Weasel", the female statues: the port has neither), Deafness branches where the port has no Deaf, the tutorial.

## Exit criteria

- A Valkyrie in a normal game finds the portal on her quest level, is called by the Norn on arriving there, is sent back below experience level 14, is assigned the quest at 14 with a pure record, descends, meets Lord Surtur, kills him for the Bell of Opening, picks up the Orb of Fate ("gotit") and returns it to the Norn ("offeredit"). A converted hero is banished and both portals are gone.
- After m24b every role has its own five levels, leader, guardians, nemesis and artifact, and the portal.
- A mirror freezes a floating eye and kills Medusa; a crystal ball finds objects of a class; lenses help searching.
- `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI; replays and the golden test are deterministic, with explained golden updates in step 2 (three new random tools) and step 3 (the quest dungeon's draws at new game, Ludios's depth).

## Testing

- Unit tests: the new rows against monsters.h and objects.h (converter test), the quest role table against role.c, the generated quest files against the converter, the ui modifiers, `questParams()`, the layout (quest branch on Oracle+6..7, its depth equal to the portal level's, 5-6 levels, Ludios 18-21), the role overlay (the hero's leader peaceful, another role's leader not changed).
- Scenario tests, outcome-based with pinned monsters and searched seeds: the portal both ways (a rectangle branch region too), the main dungeon's portal on an ordinary level, the big room and the Rogue level, each leader outcome, the expulsion and the banishment, the descent gate, each arrival message, the nemesis's death and the Bell, the artifact's return by hand and thrown, each role's five levels built (leader, guardians, nemesis with the Bell, the named artifact, stairs and portal).
- The soak bot does not reach the quest; each batch adds a build test of its roles' levels over several seeds with the invariants (no monster or object in rock, one portal on the home level, the goal's artifact present).
- Phone check uses wizard mode: `#levelchange`, `wiz:where`, `wiz:levelport`, `wiz:map`.
