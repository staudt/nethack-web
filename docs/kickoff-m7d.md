# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 7d (draft)

Milestone 7d picks up the wands, rings and traps that 5b left out and that need no new core contract: **teleportation** (one mechanism for the scroll, the wand, the ring and two traps), **digging** (the wand, holes and pits she makes, undiggable walls and floors), **levitation**, the rings of stealth, warning and conflict, the amulet of magical breathing, the wands of wishing, death, cancellation, undead turning and enlightenment, and the magic trap, anti-magic field and fire trap effects. It builds on 5b (wands, rings, traps, `docs/kickoff-m5b.md`), 6a/6b (the dungeon and `LevelDef` format, the Watch, shops), 7b (boulders, pits, holes, the scroll of earth, Sokoban, the Oracle, `docs/kickoff-m7b.md`), 7c (Mines' End) and wizard mode (`docs/kickoff-wiz.md`). Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then propose a plan before writing any code.

Everything here is content (plus a small `ui` change for up/down zaps): no core public interface or save-format change. What needs one (invisible monsters, a pick-a-position prompt, polymorph) is a later milestone.

## Scope

1. **New objects, in `objects.h` order:** wands of enlightenment, wishing, stasis, undead turning, cancellation, teleportation, digging and death; rings of stealth, levitation, conflict, warning and teleportation; the amulet of magical breathing; the scroll of teleportation. Probabilities, prices, materials and charges as C (`rnd(3)` for wishing); they join the shuffles and the random pools (ref: include/objects.h; src/mkobj.c mksobj_init(); src/o_init.c shuffle_all()).
2. **Teleporting the hero:** `tele()` and `scrolltele()` to a random safe spot ("You feel disoriented for a moment." is the Amulet's, not ours), `teleds()` with its spot effects (traps, shop exits, room entry), `safe_teleds()`, `level_tele()` to a random level (`random_teleport_level()`; "You materialize on a different level!"), confused or cursed scrolls level-teleporting, Sokoban's `noteleport` ("A mysterious force prevents you from teleporting!", "You feel very disoriented for a moment." for level teleports) (ref: src/teleport.c tele(), scrolltele(), teleds(), safe_teleds(), teleok(), level_tele(), random_teleport_level(), noteleport_level(); src/read.c seffect_teleportation()).
3. **Teleporting monsters and objects:** the wand's beam sends monsters (`u_teleport_mon()`, `rloc()`; a priest in his temple resists; a shopkeeper taken out of his shop gets angry) and objects (`rloco()`) to random spots; zapping yourself teleports you (ref: src/zap.c bhitm(), bhito(), zapyourself() WAN_TELEPORTATION; src/teleport.c u_teleport_mon(), rloc(), rloco()).
4. **Teleport traps and level teleporters**, for the hero and monsters, in the random trap roll and on Mines' End's Catacombs; "You feel a wrenching sensation." (ref: src/trap.c trapeffect_telep_trap(), trapeffect_level_telep(); src/teleport.c tele_trap(), level_tele_trap(), mtele_trap(), mlevel_tele_trap(); src/mklev.c traptype_rnd(); dat/minend-3.lua).
5. **Teleportitis and stasis:** the ring of teleportation teleports her 1 time in 85 each turn; the wand of stasis stops teleporting on the level for a while (ref: src/allmain.c moveloop_core() `Teleportation && !rn2(85)`; src/zap.c zapnodir() WAN_STASIS).
6. **Digging with the wand:** across the level through rock, walls and doors (`zap_dig()`: corridors in rock, doorways in room walls, floor on maze levels, "The wall glows then fades." on undiggable walls), down (a hole she falls through, or a pit where the floor is hard: Sokoban's entry level, the bottom of a dungeon; "The stairs are too hard to dig in."), up (a rock falls on her head); the Watch sees her dig in town (ref: src/dig.c zap_dig(), dighole(), digactualhole(), dig_check(), watch_dig(); src/dungeon.c Can_dig_down(), Can_fall_thru()).
7. **Zapping up and down:** the direction prompt offers `<` and `>`; down zaps hit the objects under her (teleport them, revive a corpse) (ref: src/zap.c zap_updown(), weffects(), bhitpile()).
8. **Level flags:** Sokoban's `noteleport`, `hardfloor` (its entry level only), non-diggable walls and solidified stone; Mines' End's non-diggable areas; maze and cavernous levels for `zap_dig()` (ref: dat/soko*.lua, dat/minend-*.lua `des.non_diggable()`, `des.level_flags()`; src/sp_lev.c; src/mkmap.c).
9. **Levitation:** putting on the ring floats her up ("You start to float in the air!"), taking it off lets her down onto whatever trap is there; while floating she cannot reach the floor (pick up, `>`, kick, sit, fountains), floats over pits, holes, trap doors and bear traps, cannot push boulders ("You don't have enough leverage..."), and Sokoban's air currents still pull her into its pits; a cursed ring keeps her up (ref: src/trap.c float_up(), float_down(); src/do_wear.c Ring_on(), Ring_off_or_gone(); the Levitation checks of src/pickup.c, do.c, dokick.c, sit.c, fountain.c, hack.c moverock(), trap.c dotrap(), fall_through()).
10. **Stealth, warning, conflict, magical breathing:** the ring of stealth; warning digits for unseen hostile monsters nearby; conflict (monsters attack her and each other whatever their attitude, pets too, unless they resist); the amulet of magical breathing (no choking, strangling does not kill) (ref: src/display.c display_warning(), mon_warning(); src/monmove.c dochug(); src/mon.c fightm(); src/eat.c choke(); src/timeout.c nh_timeout() STRANGLED).
11. **Wands of wishing, death and cancellation:** a wish ("Unfortunately, nothing happens." with bad Luck); the death ray (monsters die unless nonliving, demons or magic resistant; she dies unless reflected); cancellation of monsters (`mcan`: no special attacks, spells or passives), of objects (enchantment, charges, potions to water, scrolls to blank paper) and of herself; a wand of cancellation put into a bag of holding blows it up (ref: src/zap.c zapnodir(), buzz() ZT_DEATH, zhitm(), zhitu(), cancel_monst(), cancel_item(), zapyourself(); src/pickup.c mbag_explodes()).
12. **Wands of undead turning and enlightenment:** undead flee and are hurt; corpses rise again (`revive()`); enlightenment lists what she is (ref: src/zap.c bhitm(), bhito(), revive(), unturn_dead(); src/insight.c enlightenment(), do_enlightenment_effect()).
13. **Magic traps, anti-magic fields and fire:** the magic trap's explosion and its twenty fates (flash and roar with monsters, a tower of flame, odd feelings, taming, uncursing), the anti-magic field's energy drain, monsters on both (ref: src/trap.c trapeffect_magic_trap(), domagictrap(), dofiretrap(), trapeffect_fire_trap(), trapeffect_anti_magic(), drain_en()).
14. **Wizard mode trap wishes:** `wiz:wish` makes a trap at her feet when the wish names one ("teleportation trap"), as wizard-mode `readobjnam()` does, so the phone check can reach every trap (ref: src/objnam.c readobjnam() wizard trap branch).

## Out of scope

Teleport control and the blessed scroll's controlled teleport (need a pick-a-position prompt, a core contract change); invisibility and see invisible (need a core perception hook to hide a monster on a visible square); polymorph of the hero, monsters and objects, polymorph traps, rings of polymorph and polymorph control, amulets of change and unchanging; statues and statue traps; webs, rolling boulder traps, land mines and `scatter()`; `#untrap`, trapped doors and boxes, `#force`; breaking wands, `explode()` and cancellation blowing up magical traps; pick-axes and mattocks, dwarves digging, conjoined pits, digging while in a pit, burying; vault teleporters, magic portals; the amulets of guarding and flying; shop damage from digging (`pay_for_damage()`, `shopdig()`); monsters using wands, scrolls and rings.

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`: wish for and use each new wand, ring, amulet and the scroll; teleport around a level and to another level; dig through a wall, down through the floor and up; float over a pit and fail to pick something up, then remove the ring over the pit; see warning digits through a wall; watch monsters fight under conflict; zap death at a monster, cancellation at a floating eye, undead turning at a corpse; wish for each new trap and step on it.
- In a normal game the new objects and traps turn up at their usual rates; Sokoban stops teleporting and its entry level stops digging down.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic (with an explained golden update for the new objects and for each new random trap kind the golden level rolls), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests: `random_teleport_level()` ranges (main dungeon, Mines, bottom levels), `safe_teleds()` never lands on water, walls, monsters or traps; `noteleport` and stasis; `zap_dig()` on room walls, rock, doors, maze levels and undiggable walls; `dighole()` hole, pit (hardfloor, bottom), stairs; float up and down over each trap kind; warning levels; conflict target choice with pinned monsters; death ray against nonliving, reflection and the hero; `cancel_item()` per class; revive a placed corpse; magic trap fates by forcing the roll (search the seed, don't depend on one).
- Scenario tests: Sokoban refuses teleports, a dug hole there is the hero's (does not count for solving), the entry level only gets a pit; a teleport trap and a level teleporter for the hero and a pinned monster; teleporting out of a shop with unpaid goods is robbery; the Catacombs have their two level teleporters.
- The soak bot runs with the new objects in the pools (teleports, digging and level changes) and keeps its invariants (no hero or monster in rock, no monster on a boulder outside Orcish Town).
- Phone check uses wizard mode (`?wizard=1`): wishes for objects and traps, levelport.
