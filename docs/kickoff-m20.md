# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 20

Milestone 20 is **artifacts**: NetHack's named weapons and treasures. The artifact table, objects that carry a name, artifacts made at random, named (`#name` an elven dagger "Sting"), dipped (Excalibur from a fountain), wished for, given by the gods for a sacrifice (`#offer`, which the port does not have yet) and at crowning; their to-hit and damage bonuses against what they are made to slay, the elemental brands, Magicbane, Vorpal Blade, the Tsurugi of Muramasa and the life drainers; touching an artifact that will not have you (the blast); what they give while carried, worn or wielded (Excalibur's searching, Sting's glow, Magicbane's magic resistance...); `#invoke`; and the places earlier milestones left waiting for artifacts (the demon princes and Excalibur or Demonbane, Demonbane against summoning, monsters preferring artifact weapons, the Wizard and quest artifacts). Eight steps. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m20.md`).

**One core change is planned** (public interface, ARCHITECTURE): `ItemComponent` gains two optional fields, `name?: string` (C's `ONAME`, a personal name) and `artifact?: DefId` (C's `oartifact`, the artifact row's id). Both are plain data and absent on every existing object, so a game saved before m20 loads unchanged (no save version bump). The artifact table itself is content (`artilist.ts`, rows `nh:excalibur` ... in `artilist.h` order), like the shop types; which artifacts exist this game and which the hero has discovered (C's `artiexist[]`, `artidisco[]`) are content state in `modData.nh.arti`, iterated in table order.

**Roles and races exist** (m22 landed before m20 started): the hero is any of the 13 roles and 5 races, with her starting alignment in core's `PlayerComponent` (`Character`). Every `Role_if()`/`Race_if()` goes through content's real accessors (`roleIs(state, 'knight')`, `raceIs(state, 'elf')`, `urole()`/`urace()` in `urole.ts`; `raceHostile()` and the rest in `race.ts`), and the artifact rows name their role and race by those keys. `hack_artifacts()` is a pure function of her role and starting alignment, not stored (`artiAlign()`/`artiRole()` in `artistate.ts`): her role's aligned gift artifact and her quest artifact take her starting alignment, and Excalibur is the Knight's only for Knights (anyone lawful may have it otherwise). So the first sacrifice gift is her role's own artifact when it exists (Mjollnir for a Valkyrie, Magicbane for a Wizard, Cleaver for a Barbarian...), the Lady of the Lake answers any lawful hero (Knights 1 dip in 6), and the quest refusal of a wish works for the roles whose quest artifact is in the table (9 of 13: the Archeologist's and Valkyrie's Orbs, the Knight's Mirror and the Monk's Eyes wait for crystal balls, mirrors and lenses).

## Scope

1. **The artifact table and artifacts as objects:** the 20 gift and wish artifacts (Excalibur to Sunsword) and the quest artifacts whose base object exists (9 of 13: the Heart of Ahriman, the Sceptre of Might, the Staff of Aesculapius, the Mitre of Holiness, the Longbow of Diana, the Master Key of Thievery, the Tsurugi of Muramasa, the Platinum Yendorian Express Card, the Eye of the Aethiopica; the two Orbs, the Magic Mirror of Merlin and the Eyes of the Overworld wait for crystal balls, mirrors and lenses); object names ("a long sword named Excalibur", "the Orb of Fate"), `the` for artifact names, artifacts made by `mksobj()`'s `artif` roll (`!rn2(20 + 10 * nartifact_exist())` weapons, `!rn2(40 + ...)` armor; `mk_artifact()`), existence and discovery, the artifact price (`arti_cost()`), the "Artifacts" part of `#discoveries`, never merging, no erosion at creation (ref: include/artilist.h; src/artifact.c mk_artifact(), artifact_exists(), artifact_origin(), exist_artifact(), nartifact_exist(), find_artifact(), discover_artifact(), undiscovered_artifact(), disp_artifact_discoveries(), arti_cost(); src/do_name.c oname(); src/objnam.c xname(), bare_artifactname(), not_fully_identified(); src/mkobj.c mksobj_init(), splitobj(); src/invent.c mergable(); src/shk.c getprice()).

## Out of scope

Angels and minions (m18's dropped step 7: there are no `A` rows, so the Angels' Sunsword and Demonbane roll and `summon_minion()` stay out; `oname()` and the table are ready for them); the quest and its artifacts' quest roles (the quest leader, the Bell, returning the artifact); crystal balls, mirrors and lenses (so the two Orbs, the Magic Mirror and the Eyes); the Amulet of Yendor and high altars (`offer_real_amulet()`); bones (no artifact "un-creation" on bones); talking artifacts (`arti_speak()`); two-weapon combat; steeds; livelog and conducts (the gnostic and wish-for-artifact conducts are not tracked); knockback (Giantslayer, Ogresmasher).

## Exit criteria

- In a normal game, random artifacts appear rarely (1 in 20 of the weapons rolled with `artif`), with "named Foo" in their names and their price in shops; a phone wish (`?wizard=1`) for "Excalibur", "Sting", "Vorpal Blade" or "Magicbane" gives the artifact, and its attacks show their messages against a genesis'd target.
- Naming an elven dagger "Sting" makes Sting, and it glows when an orc comes near; naming a long sword "Excalibur" slips.
- Sacrificing fresh corpses on a co-aligned altar lowers the prayer timeout, raises Luck and, at level 3+, eventually gives Mjollnir; a cross-aligned altar can be converted.
- Touching Stormbringer as a neutral hero blasts her; carrying Magicbane gives magic resistance; invoking Fire Brand casts a fireball.
- `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI; replays and the golden test are deterministic, with an explained golden update in step 1 (artifacts made by the `artif` roll) and wherever the plan says it may move.

## Testing

- Unit tests: the table (ids unique, every base object exists, the order and fields as `artilist.h`), `mk_artifact()` by type and by alignment (eligibility, the role first choice, skill compatibility, the fallback list), existence and discovery bookkeeping, `restrict_name()`, `spec_applies()` for every bane kind and element, `spec_abon()`/`spec_dbon()` ranges, `touch_artifact()` outcomes for the hero and monsters, the invoke timeouts, `sacrifice_value()` and `eval_offering()`.
- Scenario tests, outcome-based with pinned monsters (speed 0, asleep) and searched seeds: each special attack's message and effect, beheading on a forced natural 1, the drain lowering a monster's level, Magicbane's four effects by die roll, a monster with Vorpal Blade beheading her, Sting glowing as an orc enters warning range, the blast on pickup and wield, every invoke power, sacrifice outcomes (fresh, old, same race, unicorn three ways, cross-aligned, gift), crowning.
- The soak bot meets artifacts through the random rolls; new invariants: every object with `artifact` has a table row whose base is its def, quantity 1, and the row marked existing; no two live objects carry the same artifact.
- Phone check uses wizard mode (`?wizard=1`): `wiz:wish`, `wiz:genesis`, levelport to Minetown's altar.
