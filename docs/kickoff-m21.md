# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 21

Milestone 21 adds the two levels of `dat/dungeon.lua` that m15 left out of the main dungeon: the **Rogue level**, a tribute to Rogue on DL15-18 with its 3x3 rooms, capital-letter monsters, old symbols and the ghost of a fallen Rogue player, and **Fort Ludios**, the one-level branch reached by a magic portal hidden in a vault, with its moat, soldiers, dragons, treasury and Croesus on his throne. Six steps. It builds on m15 (the main dungeon's layout), m12 (vaults and the guard, special rooms), m17 (magic portals, `des.*` level ports) and m18/m19. It comes before m20 (artifacts, parked), so nothing here uses artifacts. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m21.md`).

Steps 1-5 are content only. **Step 6 is the only core change**, and it is costly to undo: the Rogue level's symbols and colours cross the view boundary as a symbol set that core resolves into the snapshot's legend (`SymsetDef`, `LevelDef.symset`, `ViewSnapshot.symset`), and its doorways block diagonal moves through a LevelDef flag that core's cell rules read (plan Q1, Q2). If those are parked, the milestone ends after step 5 and DEVIATIONS says how the Rogue level still looks. A game saved before m21 keeps its old layout (no Rogue level, no Ludios) and loads unchanged.

## Scope

1. **Placement in `nh:main` as dungeon.lua does it:** the rogue level (base 15, range 4) first in the main dungeon's special levels, and the Fort Ludios dungeon (one level, `knox`) after Sokoban with its branch rolled on DL18-21, which sets its depth (19-22) but leaves no stairs: the real entrance is a portal placed later (ref: dat/dungeon.lua; src/dungeon.c init_dungeons(), init_level(), place_level(), init_dungeon_set_depth(), the knox "floating entrance" kludge).
2. **The Rogue level's map:** three by three sections each holding a room or a crossing, joined by a maze walk with extra links, doorways with no doors (some secret), corridors with secret squares, stairs in rooms; at any map size, exact at 80x21 (ref: src/extralev.c makeroguerooms(), miniwalk(), roguecorr(), roguejoin(), corr(); src/mklev.c makelevel() rogue path, dosdoor(), generate_stairs()).
3. **The Rogue level's generation:** ordinary room fill without fountains, sinks, altars, graves, statues, chests or graffiti; Rogue's object classes and trap kinds; only capital-letter random monsters (and polymorph forms, shapeshifters, `nasty()`), made without weapons or armour; the tribute: a sleeping ghost with a mace or two-handed sword, bow and arrows, ring or plate mail, food and maybe a cheap plastic imitation of the Amulet; "You enter what seems to be an older, more primitive world." (ref: src/extralev.c makerogueghost(); src/mklev.c fill_ordinary_room(), traptype_roguelvl(); src/mkobj.c rogueprobs; src/makemon.c rndmonst_adj(), m_initweap(), m_initinv(); src/mon.c pick_animal(), select_newcham_form(); src/wizard.c nasty(); src/do.c goto_level()).
4. **The Rogue level in play:** no tunnelling, no ranged attacks, no corpses; found secret doors are doorways; locking magic raises a wall in a doorway; opening magic says "You feel a draft."; light fills the whole room; no boulders from the scroll of earth (ref: src/monmove.c, src/mon.c LEVEL_SPECIFIC_NOCORPSE and mfndpos(), src/mhitu.c, src/detect.c cvt_sdoor_to_door(), src/lock.c, src/zap.c, src/read.c litroom() and the scroll of earth, src/muse.c).
5. **Fort Ludios:** knox.lua whole: the fort and its moat, the throne room with Croesus (a new monster), the treasury of gold with spiked pits and land mines, the barracks, the welcoming zoo, sixteen soldiers and a lieutenant, a stone giant, four dragons, four giant eels, the corner towers' gems, no teleport, no digging through its walls, no new monsters in the treasury; "You have penetrated a high security area!" and the alarm that wakes the fort while Croesus lives; level teleports going nowhere in a one-level branch; "Fort Ludios" on the status line (ref: dat/knox.lua; src/do.c goto_level(); src/dungeon.c single_level_branch(); src/teleport.c level_tele(), random_teleport_level(); src/mklev.c mktrap(); src/trap.c maketrap(); src/muse.c m_next2m(); src/mkmaze.c is_exclusion_zone(); src/botl.c describe_level(); src/makemon.c m_initinv() Croesus).
6. **The portal from a vault:** a vault made on DL11 to above Medusa may hold the hidden portal to Ludios (1 time in 3 for each eligible vault, until one is made); the portal back from the fort's arrival room; the vault guard and Croesus ("Back from the dead, are you?  I'll remedy that!" once Croesus is dead); wizard mode's `#wizwhere` names the portal level (ref: src/mklev.c mk_knox_portal(), makelevel() do_vault block, place_branch(); src/vault.c invault(); src/mkmaze.c mkportal()).
7. **The Rogue level's look (step 6, core):** Rogue's symbols (stairs `%`, doorways `+`, armour `]`, amulets `,`, food `:`, gold `*`) and no colour, switched per level, with the snapshot naming the symbol set; doorways that cannot be passed diagonally, for the hero, travel and monsters (ref: src/symbols.c init_rogue_symbols(); src/drawing.c def_r_oc_syms[]; src/display.c reset_glyphmap() GMAP_ROGUELEVEL; src/do.c goto_level() assign_graphics(); src/hack.c doorless_door(); src/mon.c mfndpos()).

## Out of scope

The Quest and its portal, the Elemental Planes, Vlad's Tower; `rogue_vision()` (the Rogue level uses ordinary vision) and blank remembered dark floor; monster names (the tribute ghost stays unnamed); bones; artifacts; Ludios's achievement and `#overview`; monsters made wand-experienced on Ludios; Sokoban's real monster-generation exclusions (it keeps its stand-in); a core portal branch type (`stairs: 'none'`, for the Quest).

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1` I can levelport to the Rogue level, see 3x3 rooms with doorways, only capital-letter monsters and the ghost's pile, and (after step 6) Rogue's symbols without colour that switch back when I leave; I cannot step diagonally through its doorways.
- With `?wizard=1` I can find the Ludios portal in a vault on DL11 or deeper (`#wizwhere` names its level), walk onto it, hear the alarm, see "Fort Ludios" on the status line, find Croesus on his throne and the treasury, fail to teleport, and walk back through the portal into the vault.
- Telling the vault guard my name is "Croesus" sends him away while Croesus lives and makes him attack once Croesus is dead.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic, with an explained golden update when the two levels first appear in the layout (verified by taking them out); `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests: layout placement over many seeds (Rogue level on DL15-18, Ludios's depth 19-22, no stairs to Ludios); `makeRogueRooms()` against a hand-traced C run with a scripted RNG, at 80x21, at a larger size and the small-map fallback; the vault portal's eligibility (depth range, one per game, wizard mode); core symset resolution and validation (step 6).
- Scenario tests: the Rogue level's rooms all joined, doorways only, its fill and trap kinds, uppercase monsters without gear, the ghost and its pile, no corpses, no tunnelling or ranged attacks, the door and light rules; Ludios built with Croesus and the treasury, exclusions honoured, the alarm, teleport rules; the portal both ways; the guard's Croesus answers; the Rogue legend and diagonal doorways (step 6). Tests search seeds or pin monsters for the case they need.
- The soak bot runs on the Rogue level (`specialStartMod`) and on Ludios (wizard levelport start). New invariants: no door square on a Rogue level has a door; at most one Ludios portal in the main dungeon, inside a vault, matching `modData.nh.knox`.
- Phone check uses wizard mode (`?wizard=1`: `wiz:where`, `wiz:levelport`, the wizard map and teleport).
