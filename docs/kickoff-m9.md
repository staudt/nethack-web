# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 9 (draft)

Milestone 9 ports the **damage types (`AD_*`) and attack styles (`AT_*`)** that the roster does not use yet, in all three directions: monster vs hero (`mhitu.c`), monster vs monster (`mhitm.c`) and the hero's side (`uhitm.c`: the passive responses, touching and wielding a cockatrice corpse, fighting from inside an engulfer). It covers gazes, exploding monsters, hugs, erosion, theft, engulfing and digestion, breath and spit, stoning, level drain and lycanthropy. It adds only one or two vanilla monsters per mechanic (real rows from the converter, `docs/CONVERTER.md`) so the phone check can reach them; the monster batch is m10. It builds on m7a (monster spells), m7d/m7e (`explode()`, `tele()`, erosion, `destroy_items()`) and the 3.7 `mhitm_data` shape already cited in `mhitu.ts`/`mhitm.ts`. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m9.md`).

Everything here is content, plus a small `ui` change (the engulf box drawn around the hero, as 7e's explosion box) and one testkit invariant exemption (the engulfer shares the hero's cell, as in C). No core contract change and no save-format change: new state lives in `modData.nh` (open questions). Effects that need hero polymorph go to 7f (see "Handed to 7f").

## Scope

1. **One damage-type dispatcher.** C 3.7's `mhitm_adtyping()` with one function per `AD_*` holding its mhitu and mhitm branches (the uhitm branch, which only a polymorphed hero reaches, is left as a `7f` note); `mattacku()` and `mattackm()` get C's full `AT_*` switch with `sum[]` (ref: src/uhitm.c mhitm_adtyping(), mhitm_ad_*(); src/mhitu.c mattacku(), hitmu(); src/mhitm.c mattackm(), hitmm(), mdamagem()).
2. **Status and erosion damage:** rust, corrosion and rot of armor (`hurtarmor()`) and monster armor, disenchantment, blinding, confusion, hallucination, wounded legs, slowing, energy drain, teleporting you or the defender, terminal illness (`diseasemu()`, non-vomitable sickness), curses (`attrcurse()`); the passives that rust, corrode and disenchant your weapon (ref: src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_rust/corr/dcay/ench/blnd/conf/halu/legs/slow/dren/tlpt/dise/curs(), passive(); src/mhitm.c passivemm(); src/mhitu.c hurtarmor(), diseasemu(), u_slow_down(); src/sit.c attrcurse()).
3. **Gazes, exploding monsters and hugs:** `gazemu()`/`gazemm()` (fire, cold, sleep, confusion, stun, blinding, slowing; Medusa's stoning with step 6), `explmu()`/`explmm()` (yellow and black lights, the spheres), `AT_BOOM` (the gas spore's death explosion through `explode()`), `AT_HUGS` (automatic after two hits, being crushed, held) and `AD_WRAP` (wrapped by a python or eel; drowning waits for pools) (ref: src/mhitu.c gazemu(), explmu(); src/mhitm.c gazemm(), explmm(); src/mon.c corpse_chance() gas spore; src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_wrap(), mhitm_ad_phys() AT_HUGS).
4. **Theft:** leprechauns stealing gold (`stealgold()`) and dodging you afterwards, nymphs stealing an item and teleporting away, charming you out of worn armor (`stealarm()` after the delay), monkeys snatching an item; monsters robbing monsters (ref: src/steal.c stealgold(), steal(), stealarm(), mpickobj(); src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_sgld(), mhitm_ad_sitm(), mhitm_ad_sedu(), mhitm_ad_ssex(); src/monmove.c m_move() leprechaun avoidance; src/fountain.c dowaternymph()).
5. **Engulfing and digestion:** `gulpmu()` (swallowed: the engulfer's damage type each turn, `u.uswldtim`, total digestion by a purple worm, being expelled), fighting, zapping and throwing from inside, the engulfer dying or being killed, `gulpmm()` for monsters swallowing monsters; the view while engulfed (ref: src/mhitu.c gulpmu(), expels(), gulp_blnd_check(); src/mhitm.c gulpmm(), engulf_target(); src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_dgst(); src/hack.c domove() u.uswallow; src/zap.c weffects()/buzz() u.uswallow; src/dothrow.c throwit() u.uswallow; src/display.c swallowed()).
6. **Breath and spit:** monsters breathing rays at range (`breamu()`: fire, cold, sleep, lightning, magic missile, poison gas, acid, and `AD_RBRE`), spitting venom (`spitmu()`: acid and blinding venom objects), and the same at other monsters (`breamm()`, `spitmm()`) (ref: src/mthrowu.c breamu(), spitmu(), breamm(), spitmm(); src/zap.c buzz() monster types, zhitu(); include/objects.h ACID_VENOM, BLINDING_VENOM).
7. **Stoning:** cockatrice and chickatrice touch and hiss (delayed stoning, `Stoned` and `stoned_dialogue()`), their passive against your bare hands and feet, touching, picking up and wielding their corpses (instant petrification; a wielded corpse stones what it hits), eating one, monsters stoned (`minstapetrify()`, `monstone()` leaving a statue with the monster's inventory), monsters attacking a cockatrice, cures (a lizard or acidic corpse, prayer, stone resistance), Medusa's gaze (ref: src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_ston(), passive() AD_STON, hmon_hitmon_misc_obj(); src/mhitu.c gazemu() AD_STON; src/timeout.c stoned_dialogue(); src/potion.c make_stoned(); src/eat.c fix_petrification(), cprefx(); src/trap.c instapetrify(), minstapetrify(); src/mon.c monstone(), mon_to_stone(); src/pickup.c, src/wield.c cant_wield_corpse(); src/pray.c TROUBLE_STONED).
8. **Level drain and lycanthropy:** `losexp()` ("Goodbye level N.", the hit points and energy gained at that level, death at level 0) from vampires' and wraiths' bites and touches, monsters draining monsters, the wraith corpse; lycanthropy from were bites and were corpses (`set_ulycn()`, "You feel feverish."), its cures (prayer, holy water), were monsters changing form and summoning help (`were_change()`, `new_were()`, `were_summon()`) (ref: src/exper.c losexp(); src/uhitm.c mhitm_ad_drli(), mhitm_ad_were(); src/were.c; src/eat.c cpostfx() PM_WRAITH, cprefx() weres; src/pray.c TROUBLE_LYCANTHROPE; src/potion.c holy water).

## Handed to 7f (polymorph-dependent)

The hero's own attacks as a monster (every `magr == &youmonst` branch, `hmonas()`, `gulpum()`, `explum()`), `AD_POLY`, the hero turning into a were creature (`you_were()`, `you_unwere()` polymorph part), turning into a green slime, a polymorphed hero being stoned or digested, golems' stone/flesh changes, vampires shapeshifting. A stoned monster leaves a plain statue object here; 7f-c gives statues their display, statue traps and level generation.

## Out of scope

The Riders' `AD_DETH`/`AD_PEST`/`AD_FAMN` (no Astral Plane), `AD_SAMU` (no Amulet of Yendor), `AT_MAGC` ray casters (`buzzmu()`, the m7a list), drowning and eels in water (no pools yet), gremlins multiplying in water, monster use of items (muse.c), steeds. `AD_HUNG` does not exist in 3.7 (hunger comes from Famine's `AD_FAMN` only).

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`: create each new monster with wizard genesis and meet its attack: a rust monster rusts your armor, a quantum mechanic teleports you, a gas spore explodes when killed, a yellow light blinds you, a pyrolisk's gaze burns, an owlbear hugs you, a leprechaun steals gold and a water nymph an item, a fog cloud engulfs you and you kill it from inside, a purple worm swallows a monster, a winter wolf cub breathes frost, a cobra spits, a chickatrice hisses (you start turning to stone and eat a lizard corpse), you wield a cockatrice corpse with gloves and stone a monster, a wraith drains a level, a wererat makes you feverish and prayer cures it.
- In a normal game the new monsters turn up at their usual depths and their attacks work against pets and other monsters too.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic (with an explained golden update for any cause the plan names), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests per damage type in both directions (a pinned monster against the hero, a pinned monster against a pinned monster), with resistance, cancellation (`mcan`) and magic cancellation (`magic_negation()`) each where C checks them; outcomes by forcing the roll or searching a seed, never lucky seeds.
- Scenario tests: a leprechaun steals and teleports, then avoids you; a nymph charms off a cloak over turns; being swallowed until expelled or digested; a gas spore's chain explosion; a cockatrice corpse wielded with and without gloves; stoning cured by a lizard corpse at each dialogue stage; level drain to death at level 1; a were rat changing form and summoning rats.
- The soak bot runs with the new monsters (wizard genesis on deep levels) and keeps its invariants (the engulfer on the hero's cell is the only shared cell).
- Phone check uses wizard mode (`?wizard=1`): genesis, wishes, levelport.
