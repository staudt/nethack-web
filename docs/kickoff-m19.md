# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 19

Milestone 19 is **tools and potions**: the objects the ROADMAP keeps pointing at from other milestones. Candles that burn and light the room, the magic lamp and the djinni inside it (and the ones in smoky potions, with the ghost in milky ones), the potion of oil (light, fuel, fire bombs), `#dip` into potions with NetHack's alchemy, holy and unholy water and dilution, tins with the tin opener and the tinning kit, the horn of plenty, the musical instruments m15 left out (flutes, horns, harps, drums with the earthquake), magic markers (writing scrolls and spellbooks, graffiti) and towels with slippery fingers (Glib). Eight steps. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m19.md`).

Almost everything is content; **no core change is planned**. Every new per-object state has a C field the port already carries: candle, lamp and oil fuel in `age`, the lit state in `lit`, the djinni, charges, ink, wetness and a tin's variety in `spe`, a tin's monster in `corpseOf`, and a diluted potion in `erosion.e1` exactly as C's `#define odiluted oeroded` (so `mergable()` already keeps diluted and undiluted potions apart). Slippery fingers are a content timed property; the open tin and its timer are content state in `modData.nh`. A game saved before m19 has none of the new objects and loads unchanged.

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`:
  - a wished stack of candles lights the room around her (more candles, wider light), burns down with its warnings and is gone; a lit potion of oil thrown at a monster explodes in flames;
  - a wished magic lamp burns forever; `#rub` it (wielded) until the djinni comes out; quaffing smoky potions sometimes frees one;
  - `#dip` a long sword into holy water (it glows light blue), a potion of healing into a potion of speed (extra healing), a scroll into water (blank), a potion twice into water (diluted, then water), an oil lamp into a potion of oil (filled);
  - open a tin with fingers and with a tin opener; make a tin from a corpse with a tinning kit; spinach makes her stronger;
  - play a magic flute (monsters fall asleep), a fire horn at a monster, a magic harp (a pet), a drum of earthquake (pits);
  - write a scroll of identify with a magic marker on blank paper; engrave graffiti with it;
  - a cursed towel makes her fingers slippery and she drops her weapon; a clean towel wipes them and a cream pie off her face.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic. Every golden change has a verified cause in its commit (expected in step 1, possibly steps 2, 3, 5 and 6), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests per mechanic, asserting outcomes: the alchemy table for every pair (`mixtype()`), dilution twice to water, each holy/unholy water case, the explosion odds bound; candle radius by count, the burn phases and fuel kept on snuffing, split and merged lit stacks keeping one timer each; the magic lamp using no fuel; each djinni outcome by BUC (a frequency test with a tolerance, or forced draws); each tin variety's nutrition and message, opening times by tool, the tinning kit's refusals; each instrument's effect on pinned monsters (speed 0, asleep) in and out of range; writing known, unknown and too-hard texts and the ink bounds; Glib dropping a wielded weapon and a towel curing it.
- Seed tests moved by the new rows are re-fitted by seed search or pinning, not weakened, and each re-fit is checked with the step's change switched off (the rows filtered, the roll not made).
- The soak bot carries the new tools (a soak that gives her candles, a magic lamp, oil, instruments, a marker and towels) and keeps the standard invariants plus: every lit object has a light and (but the magic lamp) one live burn record; no lit object is inside a container; no candle stack is empty.
