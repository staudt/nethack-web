# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 23

Milestone 23 is **the Amulet and the endgame**: everything between the Wizard's Tower and the end of a winning game. The Angels and the gods' minions; Vlad's Tower with Vlad and the Candelabrum of Invocation; the Bell of Opening (as an object: the quest that holds it is m24); Moloch's Sanctum with its high priest and the Amulet of Yendor; the invocation ritual on the vibrating square; carrying the Amulet (the mysterious force, the Wizard of Yendor's harassment and resurrection, the covetous monsters that want it); the Elemental Planes (Earth, Air with its clouds, Fire, Water with its bubbles, the Astral Plane with its three high altars, high priests, Angels, player monsters and the Riders); and ascension by offering the Amulet. Ten steps in two parts: **m23a** (steps 1-6, the Amulet) and **m23b** (steps 7-10, the Planes and ascension). Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m23.md`).

**No core logic changes; two small core edits are planned.** (1) Step 2 adds two dungeons to the vanilla layout (Vlad's Tower and the Elemental Planes), so `SAVE_VERSION` goes from 9 to 10 and older saves are refused (the user's rule: old saves are wiped, not migrated); replays and fixtures are regenerated. (2) `LevelContent` (a LevelDef's contents entry) gains two optional fields that content interprets, as `asleep` and `appearAs` do: `align` (des.monster's `align`: an aligned priest or minion made by `mk_roamer()`) and `waiting` (des.monster's `waiting`: STRAT_WAITFORU). Everything else is content: the endgame's dungeon uses the existing `DungeonDef` format (its stairs are lifted by the Planes' fixups, as Fort Ludios's), the Water and Air levels' moving bubbles and clouds are content state moved by the `endOfTurn` hook, and ascension is a content end (`endHow` 'ascended') under core's existing 'escaped' kind.

**The Bell of Opening before the quest.** NetHack gives the Bell to the quest nemesis, and a wish for it outside wizard mode gives an ordinary bell. Until m24 the Bell exists only through a wizard-mode wish, so in a normal game the invocation, the Sanctum, the real Amulet and the Planes are out of reach (they are reached and phone-checked in wizard mode). m24 places the Bell on the nemesis and nothing in m23 changes.

**Roles, artifacts and Gehennom exist.** Roles and races are m22's (`roleIs()`, `urole()`, the starting alignment in core's `Character`); the artifact table, `oname()`, `#offer` and crowning are m20's (the Angels' Sunsword or Demonbane roll uses them); the eleven Gehennom levels, the vibrating square, the Wizard of Yendor and the Book of the Dead are m17's; vampires and covetous tactics m18's.

## Exit criteria

- In wizard mode (`?wizard=1`): Vlad's Tower is reached by its stairs up from Gehennom; Vlad carries the Candelabrum; seven candles attach and light; a wished Bell of Opening rung on the vibrating square and the Book of the Dead read there open the stairs to the Sanctum; Moloch's high priest holds the Amulet; picking it up grants the wish; climbing with it, the mysterious force acts and the Wizard comes back; DL1's up stairs lead to Earth; the portals lead through Air (clouds move), Fire, Water (she is carried in her bubble) to the Astral Plane; offering the Amulet on her own god's high altar ends the game ascended, with the score doubled and "ascended to demigod-hood" in the score list.
- In a normal game an angry god sends a minion, and random Angels carry their swords.
- `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI; replays and the golden test are deterministic, with an explained golden update in step 2 (the two new dungeons' draws at new game) and wherever the plan says it may move.

## Testing

- Unit tests: the new rows against monsters.h/objects.h (converter test), the dungeon layouts (Vlad's branch on Gehennom 9-13, Planes depths and entry), `summon_minion()` by alignment, `peace_minded()` for minions and with the Amulet, `level_difficulty()` (with and without the Amulet, in the endgame), the mysterious force's odds, `mkinvokearea()`'s result, `intervene()`'s cases, bubble shapes and movement keeping every bubble inside its bounds.
- Scenario tests, outcome-based with pinned monsters (speed 0, asleep) and searched seeds: each level built (every possible special level holding Vlad's branch stairs), the Candelabrum, the invocation, the Sanctum's priest and the Amulet, the wish, stealing the Amulet, resurrection, the endgame entry, each portal, a hero carried by her bubble, Astral's altars and priests, the Riders' attacks and revival, each `offer_real_amulet()` outcome and the ascended end.
- The soak bot does not reach the endgame; a new wizard-mode soak (`soak.endgame`) starts a hero with the Amulet on each Plane and plays 300 turns with the invariants (bubbles in bounds, no monster or object in rock).
- Phone check uses wizard mode: `wiz:wish`, `wiz:levelport`, `wiz:genesis`.
