# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 17

Milestone 17 is **Gehennom**. m15 left a stub below the Castle: 20 to 24 plain maze levels entered by the Castle's trap doors. This milestone gives it NetHack's layout and levels: the Valley of the Dead with Moloch's temple and its graveyards, `hellfill`'s lava caves, iron-bar and lava mazes and cold icy mazes with their prefabs, fire traps, the level temperature, the vibrating square on the level above the bottom (the Sanctum stays out of reach, as it is before the invocation), Juiblex's swamp, Asmodeus's and Baalzebub's lairs, Orcus Town, the Wizard's Tower and the two fake towers. The monsters those levels place come with them: hell hounds, lemures, horned and barbed devils, the four demon princes (summoning, the demon court, Asmodeus's and Baalzebub's bribes), covetous monsters that wait, harass and heal on the stairs, master liches and arch-liches, shades and skeletons, and the Wizard of Yendor with the Book of the Dead. Eight steps. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m17.md`).

There are core changes, all additive and explained in `ARCHITECTURE.md`: `SpecialLevelDef.chain` (the Wizard's Tower's levels count from wizard1, dungeon.lua's `chainlevel`), the variant draw made only for special levels with several variants (as NetHack's `rndlevs`), two exported maze builders for the content-built `hellfill`, and a fixed arrival spot for `goToLevel` (ladders and magic portals). There is no save-format change: the layout is in the save, so a game started before m17 keeps its stub Gehennom. Every Gehennom special level is placed in step 1 and built as a filler level until its own step lands, so the new-game draws change once and each level becomes reachable as soon as it exists.

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`:
  - `wiz:where` shows Gehennom's special levels; arriving on the Valley gives its three lines and its down stairs ask "Are you sure you want to enter?";
  - the Valley's temple of Moloch, graveyards and corpses are there; a fire trap burns her;
  - ordinary Gehennom levels vary (lava caves, iron-bar mazes, cold mazes: "It is cold here."); the level above the bottom has no down stairs and a vibrating square; a level teleport to the bottom says "Sorry..." outside wizard mode;
  - a genesised demon gates in another; Asmodeus is invisible and asks for a bribe; Juiblex's fountains include mimics; Orcus Town's shops have no keepers; a shade shrugs off iron;
  - the fake tower's portal leads into the Wizard's Tower, its ladders connect the three levels, and the Wizard sleeps on the Book of the Dead.
- In a normal game Gehennom has 20 to 24 levels with the Valley on top, the special levels where dungeon.lua puts them and the Sanctum out of reach.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic. Every golden change has a verified cause in its commit (expected in step 1, possibly steps 2, 4, 7 and 8), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests for the layout (every range, the chained tower levels, backtracking), the bottom rule at each site, the vibrating square, each level building on 80x21 with its fixed contents, stairs and regions (each hell and prefab picked by seed search), the selection functions and `hell_tweaks()`, the temperature messages and potions, fire traps on the hero and monsters, each new monster's attacks, summoning, the demon court, each covetous strategy, the bribe's outcomes, the new spells, shades, ladders and portals both ways, the Wizard's clone and the Book's three outcomes. Use searched seeds or pinned monsters (speed 0, asleep), never lucky ones.
- Seed-dependent tests moved by the new-game draws (step 1) and the new rows are re-fitted by seed search or pinning, not weakened, and each re-fit is checked with the step's behaviour switched off.
- A soak (`soak.gehennom`) runs the bot on Gehennom levels and keeps the standard invariants.
