# Kickoff: NetHack TypeScript port, milestone 16

Milestone 16 is **engravings and Elbereth**. The hero can write on the floor with `E`: in the dust with her fingers, engraved with a blade (which dulls it, except an athame), a hard gem or a hard ring, burned with a wand of fire or lightning, dug with a wand of digging, or with any other wand, whose message tells her what it is. Engravings are read when she steps on them or looks (`:`), felt when she is blind, smudged when she walks or fights on them, scuffed by monsters and wiped by down zaps. A monster that respects it will not attack her in melee while she stands on an intact "Elbereth", and attacking from it erases it ("You feel like a hypocrite."). Levels get random graffiti (rumors and the engrave file), the vault's "ad aerarium", the shop's "Closed for inventory", the wine cellar's signs, and the Elbereths under the Sokoban prize and the Castle's wand of wishing. Last, if there is budget, graves with headstones and epitaphs. Seven steps. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `DEVIATIONS.md` first, then the plan (`~/.claude/plans/m16.md`).

There is no core change. Engravings are content state in `modData.nh.engr` (level id, then cell index), like the m15 drawbridges and the melting ice, so there is no save-format change: old saves have no engravings. Engravings are not drawn on the map (5.0's `` ` `` engraving symbol would need a core view channel, which is left to the user; see the plan's open question 2). Engraving texts are data strings in the state (typed, or picked from NetHack's rumors, engrave and epitaph files), not message keys.

## Scope

1. **Engravings and reading them:** the six engraving types (dust, engraved, burned, graffiti, blood, headstone), one per square; reading on arrival and with a new always-offered `:` "Look here" action, in look_here()'s order (the feature, the engraving, the objects); feeling engraved, burned and headstone text when blind; the run stops on a read (ref: include/engrave.h; src/engrave.c engr_at(), sengr_at(), make_engr_at(), del_engr_at(), read_engr_at(), engr_can_be_felt(), can_reach_floor(); src/pickup.c pickup(1); src/invent.c dolook(), look_here()).
2. **`E` engrave:** the stylus menu (fingers first, then weapons, wands, gems and rings suggested, the rest allowed); u_can_engrave()'s refusals (engulfed, water, lava, fountains, clouds, walls, no hands, overloaded); no free hand; can't reach the floor; an altar's wrath; blades that dull (one point per two letters; a stack splits off one), athames that don't, Fire Brand if it exists; hard gems and rings engrave; boots write in the dust; large objects, food, scrolls and books refuse; adding to or overwriting the current engraving; the text prompt; scrambled letters when confused, stunned, blind, hallucinating or in dust; engraving as an occupation (10 letters an action, 1 when carving with a blade, gem or ring), stopped by teleporting or losing the stylus; blood for a demon form (ref: src/engrave.c doengrave(), doengrave_sfx_item(), doengrave_ctx_verb(), engrave(), stylus_ok(), u_can_engrave(), freehand()).
3. **Engraving with wands:** zappable() charges, wresting, cancelled wands turning to dust, cursed wands exploding; every wand's message ("The bugs on the floor stop moving!", "The engraving now reads: ...", "Gravel flies up from the floor.") and the auto-identification of digging, fire and lightning; non-directional wands zapping (light, secret door detection, stasis, create monster, wishing, enlightenment); polymorph, cancellation, make invisible, teleportation and cold acting on the old engraving; the lightning flash blinding her; a wand's gesture when she floats. Down zaps and spells hitting an engraving (polymorph, cancellation, make invisible, teleportation, stone to flesh, striking, force bolt) and a monster's magic missile spell on her square (ref: src/engrave.c doengrave_sfx_item_WAN(), rloc_engr(); src/zap.c zappable(), zapnodir(), zap_map(), mon_spell_hits_spot()).
4. **Smudging and wiping:** walking smudges the squares she leaves and enters; fighting (3), throwing (2), kicking (2), applying a polearm, and digging down scuff her square; one roll a turn `!rn2(40 + 3 * Dex)` scuffs it too; every moving monster scuffs its own square; burned engravings resist (except on ice or to magic half the time), headstones and protected engravings never wear. Terrain changes delete what lies under them (pits and holes, land mines, gushing fountains, swamps, drawbridges, the vault guard's corridor, shop repairs, terrain wishes) (ref: src/engrave.c wipe_engr_at(), u_wipe_engr(), wipeout_text(); src/hack.c domove(), maybe_smudge_engr(); src/uhitm.c attack(); src/dothrow.c throw_obj(); src/apply.c use_pole(); src/monmove.c dochug(); src/allmain.c moveloop_core()).
5. **Elbereth:** onscary() as 5.0 has it: an intact, finished "Elbereth" (exact, any case, not a headstone) scares a monster when the hero stands on it (or on her displaced image), or when a level-made Elbereth has objects on it; humans, uniques, shopkeepers, guards, blind, peaceful and minotaurs ignore it, and it has no power in Gehennom. Scared monsters flee and do not attack in melee; they do not step onto it, choose it as a goal, or break off the hero's occupation. Attacking a monster that respects it (or a peaceful) while standing on it: "You feel like a hypocrite.", alignment lost and the engraving erased, whatever its type (5.0 = 3.7's rule) (ref: src/monmove.c onscary(), distfleeck(), dochug(); src/mon.c setmangry(), mfndpos(); src/hack.c monster_nearby()).
6. **Engravings at level creation:** random graffiti in ordinary rooms (`!rn2(27 + 3 * depth)`, from the rumors file 3 times in 4, else the engrave file, a quarter of its letters worn), a level-made Elbereth guarding objects; the vault teleporter niche's "ad aerarium" in the dust, the locked shop door's "Closed for inventory", the Gnome King's wine cellar's two signs, the burned Elbereth and cursed scroll of scare monster on the Sokoban prize and under the Castle's wand of wishing (ref: src/engrave.c random_engraving(); src/rumors.c getrumor(), get_rnd_text(); dat/rumors.tru, rumors.fal, engrave.txt; src/mklev.c fill_ordinary_room(), makeniche(); src/shknam.c stock_room(); src/sp_lev.c lspo_engraving(); dat/minend-2.lua, soko1-1.lua, soko1-2.lua, castle.lua).
7. **Graves (if budget allows):** a grave terrain with a headstone and an epitaph (dat/epitaph.txt), graves in morgues and, rarely, in ordinary rooms; engraving on a headstone adds to the epitaph, fingers only smudge, and the first try disturbs the undead (a ghoul) (ref: src/engrave.c make_grave(), disturb_grave(); src/mklev.c mkgrave(); src/mkroom.c fill_zoo() MORGUE).

## Out of scope

The engraving symbol on the map and farlook of engravings (needs a core view channel; ROADMAP), magic markers and towels (the objects do not exist; m19 tools), make_niches() and its trap door niche ("Vlad was here"), digging graves up, buried objects and the bell, kicking or sitting on graves beyond a message, bones, the literate conduct, Elbereth placement checks for new monsters (`GP_CHECKSCARY`), Gehennom's own levels, roles and races.

## Exit criteria

- On a phone with `?wizard=1`:
  - she writes "Elbereth" in the dust with her fingers and reads it back when she steps off and on again; a jackal next to her flees and does not bite while she stands on it; she attacks it from the square: "You feel like a hypocrite." and the engraving is gone;
  - engraving with an unknown wand of digging identifies it ("This iron wand is a wand of digging!"); a wand of fire burns the text; a dagger gets dull; an athame does not;
  - blind, she feels a burned engraving but not one in the dust; walking over a dust engraving a few times wears it away; a burned one stays;
  - a random graffiti appears in a room on some new levels (levelport around); the vault teleporter's niche has "ad aerarium" in front of it; the Sokoban prize sits on a burned Elbereth.
- Replays and the golden test are deterministic. Every golden change has a verified cause in its commit (expected in steps 1, 2, 4, 6 and 7), and `pnpm check` is green locally and in CI.

## Testing

- Unit tests for every engraving rule: each type's read and felt messages, the end punctuation, wipeout_text() and its rubouts, wipe_engr_at() by type (dust, engraved, burned on floor and ice, headstone, protected), each stylus class in doengrave, the add/overwrite/cannot-wipe questions, the occupation's rates, dulling and truncation, each wand's engrave effect and identification, down zaps on an engraving, the onscary() Elbereth cases (hero on it, not on it, guarded objects, each immune kind, Gehennom), the hypocrite rule, random engravings and their placement, each special level's engraving. Use searched seeds or pinned monsters (speed 0, asleep), never lucky ones.
- Seed-dependent tests moved by the new per-turn and per-room rolls are re-fitted by seed search or pinning, not weakened, and each re-fit is checked with the step's behaviour switched off.
- A soak runs the bot with engraving (it writes "Elbereth" now and then) and keeps its invariants: no engraving on a liquid, wall, stone or tree square, no empty text, no engraving on a level that is not in the state.
